Important Information

Order Information & FAQs

Are shipping charges included?

No. Shipping is charged separately at standard applicable rates in India and internationally.

Do you offer Cash on Delivery?

No. Orders are accepted only against advance payment.

Do you offer returns or exchanges?

No. Every piece is checked before dispatch. Pictures/details can be shared before dispatch and we can coordinate on WhatsApp.

Can you make custom sizes?

Yes. Everything is made-to-measure. Standard sizing is not required.

What about sizes beyond Large?

Additional charges may apply depending on the garment. We will tell you before placing the order.

Will my measurements be confirmed?

Yes. We coordinate with you and can arrange a call before placing the order.

Can colours vary?

Slight variation can happen due to photography, lighting and screens. Please coordinate with us before ordering if exact shade matters.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes. Worldwide shipping is available and charged separately at standard applicable rates.

Size & order disclaimer: Please provide accurate measurements and review the details shared before payment. Any special size, fabric, construction or customization charge will be communicated and approved before the order is placed.

Before you order

Everything you need to know

Your order is confirmed only after we have discussed the details with you.

  • Shipping is charged separately at applicable standard rates in India and internationally.
  • All pieces are made-to-measure. Non-standard sizes are welcome.
  • Sizes above Large may have an additional charge; we will confirm this before ordering.
  • Advance payment is required. We do not offer Cash on Delivery.
  • There are no returns or exchanges. Every piece is checked carefully before dispatch.
  • Colour may vary slightly because of photography, lighting and screen settings.
  • Our team will coordinate with you before confirming measurements and order details.

For any clarification, please use the WhatsApp button at the bottom of the screen.
